*Random Sequence Generator · Last updated 16 August 2026*

**Random Sequence Generator does not collect any data.** It has no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no network access of any kind.
Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not gather, transmit, sell, or share personal information,
usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics. There is no server behind it, and the
app contains no code that opens a network connection.

## Data the app stores on your device

Sequences you explicitly choose to save — the sequence itself, the name you give
it, the mode it was generated in, and the time it was saved — are written to your
device's local app storage. This data:

- never leaves your device unless you share it yourself;
- is not readable by other apps;
- is deleted when you delete a saved entry, and deleted in full when you
  uninstall the app.

## Copying and sharing

When you use the copy or share action, the app hands the selected text to your
device's clipboard or its system share sheet, at your request. What happens next
is governed by the operating system and by whichever app you choose to share
into. Random Sequence Generator plays no part in it and receives nothing back.

## Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone,
location, contacts, photos, files, or any other protected resource.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be a
clarification rather than a new data practice.
